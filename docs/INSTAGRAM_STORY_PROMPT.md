# Instagram story prompt: how to sign up for the open mic

Paste the block below into Claude. It has every real detail already filled in,
so nothing has to be invented and nothing has to be looked up.

Re-run it whenever the format changes. If you change the slot count or the spot
length, change them here too: this file is a copy of the facts, not a live read
of them, and a story that says ten spots when the site says twelve is worse
than no story.

Make a set of five Instagram story frames in Adobe Express explaining how to
sign up for our open mic. 1080 x 1920 each.

WHO WE ARE
Stoned Goose Productions, a comedy production company in Olympia, Washington.
Instagram @stonedgooseproductions. Site stonedgooseproductions.com.

THE FACTS. Every one of these is real. Do not add prices, names, promises or
statistics that are not on this list.
- The room is the Log Cabin Comedy Open Mic, our own weekly mic.
- Every Monday at the Log Cabin Bar & Grill, 7035 Pacific Ave SE, Olympia, WA
  98503.
- Show starts at 7:00 PM. That is the only time we publish. There is no sign up
  time, no doors time and no end time. Do not add one.
- 12 spots per night, 8 minutes each.
- Spots are claimed in advance online. There is no clipboard on the night.
  Turning up without a spot means watching, not performing.
- Sign up at stonedgooseproductions.com/open-mics
- Four Mondays are open at a time. A new Monday is added every Tuesday morning.
- Signing up takes a name, an email and an Instagram handle.
- A confirmation email arrives with the spot number, and a link to release the
  spot if you cannot make it.
- Running order goes up the night of. The spot number is the order you signed
  up in, not necessarily the order you go on in.
- The new format starts Monday 28 September.
- The motto is "Sign up, show up, go up."
- Do not mention a cover charge either way. We do not publish one.

THE FIVE FRAMES
1. The change. The mic is pre sign up now. Big, loud, one idea.
2. The format. 12 spots. 8 minutes. Every Monday, 7 PM.
3. How to sign up, as three steps: go to the link, pick your Monday, leave your
   name, email and Instagram.
4. What happens next. Confirmation email with your spot number. Cannot make it,
   release your spot so somebody else can have it.
5. The call to action. Sign up at stonedgooseproductions.com/open-mics, the
   venue and address, and "Sign up, show up, go up." Leave clear space in the
   lower third for a link sticker.

BRAND. This is a specified system. Where taste and the spec disagree, the spec
wins.

Colours, and there are no others:
- Tuxedo #0F0F0F
- Ivory #F4EEE2
- Accent gold #D4AA4A
- Gold ink #87681F, for small gold text on a light ground only
- Smoke #8C8781, secondary text and hairlines, the only permitted grey

- Every frame is a tuxedo background or an ivory background, EXCEPT that
  Instagram stories are the one place a full gold background is allowed. Use it
  on at most one frame, for the loudest one. Never gold behind body text.
- Gold never carries body copy. Gold text is headline size on tuxedo. On ivory,
  small gold text must be gold ink #87681F, never accent gold, which measures
  1.88:1 and is unreadable.
- No gradients, no shadows, no glows, no glassmorphism, no photo overlays or
  tints. Flat panels only.
- No second accent colour.

Type. One typeface, Josefin Sans, weights 300, 400 and 700 only. No italics.
- Headlines: Bold 700, UPPERCASE, letterspaced about 4%.
- Subheads: Bold 700, UPPERCASE, letterspaced about 6%.
- Small labels and eyebrows: Regular 400, UPPERCASE, letterspaced about 26%.
- Body: Light 300, sentence case, normal spacing.
- Never swap those. Never condense, stretch or outline the type.

The mark. Use the lockup, the goose with the joint and the wordmark beneath,
because a story is sold to an audience. Not the circular badge, which is for
client facing material. Files:
- Gold on tuxedo: public/brand/web/SGP_Lockup_Gold.png
- Ivory on tuxedo: public/brand/web/SGP_Lockup_Ivory.png
- Tuxedo on ivory or gold: public/brand/web/SGP_Lockup_Tuxedo.png
Never recolour a mark with filters or blend modes: every colourway is its own
file. Clear space is already baked into the artwork, so do not add padding
around it. Size it by width and let the height follow, minimum 281px wide. The
lockup canvas is roughly 1.107:1, so never crop it square.

Writing. Adult Swim register: short, blunt, funny, never corporate and never
apologetic. No em dashes anywhere, use periods, commas or colons. No exclamation
marks stacked up. The goose is the fun, the type stays straight.

Layout for the format. Instagram covers roughly the top 250px and bottom 250px
of a 1920 tall frame with its own interface, so keep every word inside that.
Frame 5 needs the lower third clear for a link sticker.
